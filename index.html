Frequently asked questions

Common questions from speakers — answered by our team.
What time should I arrive at the venue? ▾
Please arrive at least 90 minutes before your session for the technical run-through, microphone check, and a brief greenroom welcome from the program director.
In which format do I submit my slides? ▾
Slides are accepted as PDF or 16:9 PowerPoint. Upload up to 48 hours before your session via the email link sent by our content team. A reminder email is sent one week before the event.
Is transportation arranged from the airport? ▾
Yes — a private transfer is reserved for each speaker. Share your flight details with our operations team and a confirmation arrives 72 hours prior to arrival.
Can I bring a guest to the gala dinner? ▾
Yes, each speaker is welcome to bring one guest. Share their name with our team and a personalized badge will be prepared at on-site registration.
Is there a dress code? ▾
Business attire is recommended for all sessions. The gala dinner is smart-casual to formal — the choice is yours.
Will my session be recorded? ▾
Yes, every session is professionally recorded. You'll receive a private link to your recording within two weeks of the event, along with a release form for any public distribution.
Can I make changes to my talk title after submission? ▾
Title and abstract revisions are accepted up to 14 days before the event. Please submit them through the Ask tab and our content team will update the public agenda.
Will there be a confidence monitor on stage? ▾
Yes, every main stage is equipped with two confidence monitors (center-stage and lectern), plus presenter notes mode for both PDF and PowerPoint.
