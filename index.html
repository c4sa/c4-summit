Frequently asked
What time should I arrive at the venue? ▾
Please arrive at least 90 minutes before your session slot for the technical run-through, microphone check, and a brief greenroom welcome from the program director.
In which format do I submit my slides? ▾
Slides are accepted as PDF or 16:9 PowerPoint. The system reserves a slot for your upload up to 48 hours before your session. The official template is in the Brand Assets section.
Is transportation arranged from the airport? ▾
Yes — a private transfer is reserved for each approved speaker. Share your flight information through the "My Session" tab and a confirmation will be sent 72 hours prior to arrival.
Can I bring a guest to the gala dinner? ▾
Yes, each speaker is welcome to bring one guest to the gala. Add your guest's name from the "My Session" tab and a personalized badge will be prepared at on-site registration.
